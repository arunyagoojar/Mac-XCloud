# Mac Xcloud 1.3.9

## New
- Keyboard and mouse now work in games that support them, the same way they do in Chrome or Edge. Click the game to capture the mouse, press Esc for the game's menu, hold Esc to release the mouse.
- Other games can be played with keyboard and mouse through a virtual controller (Standard and Shooter layouts). Click the game or press a mapped key to start.
- New Settings window, laid out like System Settings, with search and live previews for steering, aiming and the touchpad.

## Improved
- Motion steering rebuilt. Tilting the controller toward or away from you no longer steers, rumble and arm movement don't pull the wheel, and the straight-ahead position is remembered.
- Steering "Center boost" (was Game dead zone) now fades in smoothly instead of snapping across center.
- Gyro aiming rebuilt: works held flat, tilted or upright, ignores hand tremor, calibrates drift while you hold the controller, and has a Smoothing control.
- Touchpad camera works like a trackpad: precise slow strokes, faster swipes turn further, stops when the finger stops.
- Rumble plays on the matching side of the DualSense. Accelerator trigger preset is progressive.

## Fixes
- Saved game profiles from older versions that were being skipped load again.
- Trigger effect menus no longer close while you browse them.
- No more false "connection" errors after a dropped request or a cancelled page load.
- Sign Out asks for confirmation.

## Removed
- Flick shifting.
- Trigger "recoil" pulses that played whenever a trigger was held.
