# Mac Xcloud 1.4.1

## Improved
- Steering is steadier. The accelerometer now corrects drift only while the controller is moving (the approach of Jibb Smart's GamepadMotionHelpers), so a steady hold doesn't wobble, rumble no longer pulls the wheel, and quick turns stop where your hands stop. Smoothing is always on; its lightest setting still steadies hand tremor.
- Steady during rumble is automatic now, so its switch is gone.
- Adaptive triggers keep their effect. The game's trigger rumble plays in a trigger only when it has no effect of its own, otherwise you feel it in that grip. Choosing a preset again puts it back, and effects return when you come back to the app.
- Keyboard and controller take turns cleanly: a key or a click hands control to the keyboard, a button, trigger or stick takes it back. Moving the mouse or the controller alone no longer switches.
- In games with their own keyboard and mouse support, the controller's motion controls pause while you use the keyboard or mouse, until you press a button on the controller.
- Gyro drift is learned while you hold the controller again, as in 1.3.9, and Wi‑Fi checks no longer run on the main thread.
- The touchpad camera is smoother on slow strokes, and a resting finger never moves it.
- Keyboard layouts: names keep their spaces, and one damaged saved layout no longer hides the others.

## Removed
- Gyro aiming with the mouse, and feeling the game's sound as vibration.

## Fixes
- Dragging a slider in Settings could trigger "Publishing changes from within view updates".
