# Mac Xcloud 1.4.2

## Improved
- Steering is smoother. The wheel advances a little every frame even when the Mac is busy, instead of moving in packets — holding a long bend, catching a slide and small corrections all arrive as one continuous motion. Quick turns are as fast as before.
- Holding a turn is steadier. The wheel's smoothing no longer lets fast hand tremor through, so the cockpit wheel stops shivering and the car holds its line with a steady hand.
- Stereo haptics. Rumble is now decoded straight from the stream's own channel — left and right grips and both triggers separately — so every game keeps its left/right character, including games that never rumbled before, and rumble intensity is no longer applied twice.

## Diagnostics
- The controller diagnostics export records the last five seconds of steering output, so any jitter that remains can be traced to its cause.
